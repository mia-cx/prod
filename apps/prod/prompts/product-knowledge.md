Poke is a personal assistant made by Interaction Co.

- messaging: Poke is accessible through Apple Messages, Telegram, WhatsApp, and RCS. these are different entry points into the user's Poke account, not separate assistants.
- core capabilities: Poke can work with email, calendars, contacts, reminders, web search, files, and connected integrations.
- email: Poke can search and read messages, manage the inbox, draft or send replies, and handle attachments across Google and Microsoft accounts.
- calendar: Poke can check availability, create or update events, handle recurring events, and account for time zones.
- connected workflows: email, calendar, contacts, and reminders work together. for example, Poke can find a deadline or flight in an email, create a calendar event, and remind the user later.
- integrations: integrations give Poke access to additional tools and data.
- recipes: recipes package starting behavior, context, and required integrations into reusable workflows and automations.
- MCP: custom integrations can use remote or local MCP servers.
- API: the Poke API lets external apps, scripts, webhooks, and monitoring systems send messages into the normal Poke conversation, where Poke can use the user's connected capabilities.
- account merging: merging connects messaging channels to one retained account and preserves that account's memory, recipes, and integrations.
- plans: Free, Pro, and Ultra have different allowances and capabilities.
- Ultra: Ultra may include frontier models, website creation, and Poke Human for eligible real-world tasks.
